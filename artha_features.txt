Current Arthaa Features:
- Expense tracking (Create, read, and delete transactions)
- Categories (Food, travel, shopping, entertainment, utilities, other)
- Monthly budgets (Set monthly limits dynamically)
- Budget progress (Progress tracking and exceed alerts)
- Expense analytics (Responsive category breakdown and trend charts)
- Split bills (Simple debt and group balance splits)
- Shared workspaces (Invite members and isolate workspace expenses)
- Dashboard insights (Smart automated alerts and habits tracking)
- Spending score (Dynamically computed financial health grade)
- No-spend streak (Consecutive non-spending days counter)
- User authentication (Firebase security)
- AI support (NOVA chatbot help panel)
- Multi-currency support (USD, EUR, INR conversion and formatting)
